HOSPITALITY IS OUR STRATEGY.

Coyote is a boutique agency built in the image of hospitality itself. We like to see ourselves as the bartender's favorite bartender – personable, elevating one's taste, and intentional with every detail.

We built Coyote out of belief: belief that people and brands deserve to be seen at their full potential. We saw founders pouring everything into their craft, doing the work, showing up with heart – and we wanted to help them become as big as they truly are.

Coyote is the symbol of transformation. It adapts, it evolves, it moves through the world with instinct and confidence. A coyote runs in a pack, never alone, because real growth happens in community. That's what we build here: a pack of thinkers, creators, and storytellers helping brands and founders show the world just how stellar they are.

We work with hotels, restaurants, and F&B brands that understand service as an art form. Strategy, design, and growth are our mediums, and hospitality is the principle that guides everything we touch.

At Coyote, we don't just market hospitality.

We live it. It's in how we listen, how we serve, and how we help brands take up the space they've earned.

We turn ambition into experience, and experience into legacy – one deliberate detail at a time.
