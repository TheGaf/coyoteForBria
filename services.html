Our Services

At Coyote, we work hand in hand with our clients to understand what's being asked and what's really needed. We diagnose before we design, building solutions that outlast the project itself. From strategy to execution, we're in the room with you: mapping the plan, defining the players, and ensuring every detail meets the moment. Our systems are built to create clarity, momentum, and measurable transformation so your brand doesn't just move forward, it evolves.

Detailed Services

Web Development

From custom CSS to going live, we can build you a custom website to help improve sales, tell your story, and connect with your audience. We're with you throughout the entire process.

Marketing Strategy

You have a story that deserves to be told. With Coyote Agency, we make a plan for the micro and the macro to help you hit your goals both digitally and in the analog world.

Merchandise Design

A good brand always has good apparel. We've helped our partners increase sales and make an imprint on their brand identity with custom clothing designs.

Editorial & Photography

Coyote Agency started as a magazine spotlighting grass roots artists. Editorials and photoshoots are our strong suit. Capture your audience's eye with a campaign that takes their breath away.

AI & Automation

By blending AI with intentional automation, we design smarter systems that connect data, creativity, and service - turning everyday operations into elevated, effortless experiences.
